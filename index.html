I'm Ritika Sharma — a researcher, science communicator, and visual artist who believes the most powerful ideas deserve the most compelling presentation.

My work spans nanoscience research, data-driven science art, academic publishing, and public education. I write, I speak, I create — always with the goal of making complex science feel human and beautiful.
